When to Call Us

The Physical Layer Determines Everything Above It.

Most technology problems that look like software or configuration problems are actually physical layer problems — insufficient bandwidth, dead zones, improperly terminated runs, or security cameras that weren't positioned for evidentiary value. Layer8 infrastructure engagements start with a site assessment so we understand the environment before we spec anything.

→
New office or second location — spec and install the physical layer before move-in, not after
→
Renovation or buildout — cabling needs to go in before drywall, not after
→
Inherited infrastructure — unlabeled cabling, dead ports, unknown panel configs that slow down every IT change
→
Compliance or insurance requirement — CCTV, access control, or Wi-Fi segmentation required for audit or policy
Why It Matters

Infrastructure Is the Foundation. Cutting Corners Here Compounds.

A Cat5e run where Cat6A was needed. Access points positioned for coverage rather than capacity. A camera system installed for deterrence rather than identification. These decisions look cheap at install time and expensive for the next five years.

Layer8 infrastructure work is designed to hand off cleanly — to your MSP, your IT team, or our own advisory practice. Every engagement produces as-built documentation so whoever comes next isn't starting blind.

How We Work

Assess. Design. Install. Document. Hand Off.

Every infrastructure engagement follows the same sequence — because skipping the assessment or skipping the documentation is how you end up with a system nobody understands six months later.

Step 1

Site Assessment

We walk the space, document existing infrastructure, identify constraints (conduit paths, ceiling access, power locations), and scope what's needed. No spec before the walk.

Step 2

Design & Proposal

Floor plan markup, bill of materials, labor estimate, and timeline — reviewed with you before anything is ordered. We explain the design decisions so you understand what you're approving.

Step 3

Installation

Clean installation to spec, coordinated with your general contractor or facilities team where the buildout is active. We handle scheduling around your operations.

Step 4

Testing & Certification

Every run tested and certified. Wi-Fi validated with post-installation survey. Camera coverage verified against the design. Nothing signed off until it passes.

Step 5

As-Built Documentation

Labeled floor plan, port schedule, cable schedule, equipment inventory, and configuration notes — delivered at project close so your IT team or MSP isn't guessing.

Step 6

Clean Handoff

We hand off to your team, your MSP, or our own advisory practice. No lock-in, no ongoing dependency on us for day-to-day operations. You own the infrastructure and the documentation.
