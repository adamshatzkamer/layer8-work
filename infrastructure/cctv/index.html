The Problem

Most Surveillance Systems Have Cameras. Fewer Have Coverage.

Cameras aimed at the ceiling, mounted where conduit was convenient rather than where coverage is needed, running on an NVR with seven days of storage and no backup — these are the systems that produce nothing useful after an incident. Coverage requires a design. Retention requires a retention policy. Useful footage requires the right camera in the right place with the right configuration.

✗
Coverage gaps — cameras covering what was easy to cable, not entry points, blind spots, or high-value areas
✗
Inadequate retention — seven days of storage on a system that typically surfaces incidents two weeks later
✗
No network isolation — IP cameras on the same VLAN as workstations, a common compliance failure and attack surface
✓
Layer8 approach — coverage designed from a floor plan, retention policy aligned to compliance, cameras on an isolated VLAN
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for a Surveillance Engagement.

  • New office or facility buildout — surveillance designed with the rest of the infrastructure
  • Upgrading analog CCTV to IP cameras
  • Compliance requirement for video retention (HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, insurance)
  • Security incident that revealed coverage gaps or storage limitations
  • Inherited system with no documentation or management interface
  • Multi-site deployment needing centralized VMS access
  • Exterior perimeter coverage for parking structures, loading docks, or yard areas
What We Do

IP Surveillance Designed for Coverage, Retention, and Compliance.

From single-site office installations to multi-site enterprise deployments — designed to produce usable footage when you need it.

📐

Coverage Design

Floor plan-based camera placement design — field-of-view mapping, lens selection, mounting height, and lighting analysis for each camera position. Entry and exit points, high-value areas, and regulatory-required zones covered first. No placement by gut feel.

📷

IP Camera Installation

Physical installation of IP cameras — ceiling dome, bullet, PTZ, and multi-sensor — on Cat6A PoE drops or fiber runs. Interior and exterior. Weatherproofed and vandal-resistant hardware selected for the mounting environment. Cameras aimed and focused before project close-out.

🗄️

NVR / VMS Configuration

Network video recorder or enterprise VMS configuration — camera discovery, recording schedules, motion detection zones, retention policy, and remote access. Platforms include Milestone, Genetec, Hanwha Wisenet, and Ubiquiti Protect. Storage sized to your retention requirement.

🔒

Network Isolation & Security

IP cameras on a dedicated surveillance VLAN, isolated from corporate network segments. Required for PCI-DSS compliance and increasingly required by cyber insurance carriers. Firewall rules configured to allow management access without exposing camera feeds to general network traffic.

🏢

Multi-Site & Cloud-Managed

Centralized VMS access across multiple locations — view any site from a single management interface. Cloud-managed platforms for organizations without on-site IT staff. Remote health monitoring and alert configuration so a camera that goes offline doesn't stay offline unnoticed.

🔧

Audit & Remediation

Assessment of existing surveillance deployments — coverage gap analysis, retention review, firmware audit, network isolation check, and storage health. Remediation plan with prioritized gaps and upgrade path. For environments that have cameras but aren't sure they have coverage.

Compliance & Standards

Surveillance That Satisfies Your Compliance and Insurance Requirements.

30–90d
Retention periods designed to match your compliance requirement — HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, and cyber insurance questionnaire retention minimums met by design, not by accident
VLAN-Isolated
Camera traffic on a dedicated VLAN in every deployment — standard on every install, not an upgrade. Required for PCI-DSS and increasingly required by cyber insurance carriers
4K Ready
Current-generation 4K IP cameras with on-board analytics — license plate capture, facial detection, and motion zones configured as part of the system, not as afterthought add-ons
📋

Documentation That Travels with the Building

Camera schedule with location, model, IP address, and channel. Coverage map showing each camera's field of view. NVR/VMS configuration export. Network diagram showing VLAN assignment. Retention policy documentation. A package your IT team, your auditor, and your next integrator can use.
