FIBER OPTIC — INSTALLATION & TERMINATION

Fiber Installed, Terminated,
and OTDR-Tested.

Fiber optic infrastructure is the backbone that every other system runs on — switching, storage, inter-building connectivity, and high-bandwidth AV distribution. It needs to be installed correctly, terminated precisely, and tested end-to-end before anything depends on it. We do all three, and we document the results.

The Problem

Fiber That Wasn't Tested Is Fiber You're Trusting on Faith.

A fiber run that looks correct can have a bad termination, a tight bend in conduit, or a fusion splice with marginal loss — and everything works fine at 1GbE while failing intermittently at 10GbE or 40GbE. These faults are invisible without an OTDR. Testing is not an optional step. It's the only way to know what you actually have.

✗
Untested runs — fiber installed without OTDR trace, faults hidden until the first high-speed uplink fails
✗
Wrong fiber type — OM3 multimode run in a campus segment that now needs 40GbE — too lossy to work at full bandwidth
✗
No documentation — no record of fiber count, connector type, run length, or test results — impossible to troubleshoot or extend
✓
Layer8 approach — right fiber for the application, every strand OTDR-tested, full test results in the close-out package
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for a Fiber Engagement.

  • MDF-to-IDF backbone installation in a multi-floor building
  • Inter-building campus fiber between facilities on the same property
  • Data center interconnects requiring low-latency, high-bandwidth runs
  • Upgrading switching infrastructure to 10GbE or 40GbE uplinks
  • Fiber-fed AP deployments for high-density wireless environments
  • AV over IP distribution requiring fiber infrastructure
  • Existing fiber with intermittent errors or undocumented strand count
What We Do

Single-Mode, Multimode, Fusion Splice, and Pre-Terminated — Every Application.

Fiber selected and installed for the application — not whatever was on the truck. From backbone runs to data center cross-connects.

💡

Single-Mode & Multimode Installation

OS2 single-mode for long campus runs and inter-building connectivity. OM4/OM5 multimode for high-bandwidth short runs — 10GbE, 25GbE, 40GbE, and 100GbE in the data center and MDF. Fiber type selected for the distance, bandwidth, and transceiver budget — not defaulted to whatever costs less.

🔬

Fusion Splicing

Precision fusion splicing for backbone runs, conduit segments, and any installation where the fiber count or run length requires a continuous strand rather than a connector. Splice loss measured at the splicer and verified by OTDR. Low-loss mechanical splice alternative where fusion is not practical.

🔌

Termination & Polishing

Factory-quality field terminations — LC, SC, ST, and MPO/MTP connectors. Inspection-grade end-face polishing verified under 400x magnification before the connector is accepted. Terminations that meet TIA-568 insertion loss specifications, not just "close enough."

📊

OTDR Testing & Certification

Optical time-domain reflectometer testing of every run — event map showing splice points, connectors, and any anomaly. Insertion loss measured end-to-end for every strand. Pass/fail against the loss budget for the intended transceiver and distance. No run signed off without a trace.

🗄️

Fiber Distribution Frames & Enclosures

Rack-mount fiber distribution frames, wall-mount enclosures, and splice closures — organized, labeled, and protected. Bend radius maintained throughout. Fiber managed so the next technician can identify a strand, add a run, or replace a connector without disturbing the rest of the distribution.

🔧

Existing Fiber Audit & Repair

OTDR audit of inherited or undocumented fiber — identifying bad connectors, excessive splice loss, tight bends, and strand count versus what's documented. Repair or re-termination of failed strands. Documentation produced for the strands that are clean and the work performed on those that weren't.
