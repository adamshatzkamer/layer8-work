The Problem

Most Cabling Problems Were Avoidable. They Just Weren't Planned.

Cabling installed without a design ends up re-installed. Cut-rate runs fail certification. Unlabeled infrastructure becomes a wall of mystery when your IT person leaves. And a building wired for 100Mbps in 2010 doesn't support the wireless APs, IP cameras, VoIP phones, and workstations you're running today.

✗
No documentation — no one knows what goes where, what's live, or what failed a test that was never run
✗
Underspecified cable — Cat5e runs trying to carry 10GbE to a core switch or PoE to a ceiling AP
✗
No pathway plan — conduit routed where it was convenient, not where the next drop needs to go
✓
Layer8 approach — designed first, certified on completion, documented for whoever comes next
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for a Cabling Engagement.

  • New office buildout or tenant improvement
  • Renovation requiring cabling to be moved or extended
  • Inherited infrastructure with no records or documentation
  • Adding IP cameras, APs, or VoIP — running out of ports
  • Upgrading to 10GbE switching and finding Cat5e everywhere
  • Building permit or lease requiring certified structured cabling
  • M&A diligence surfacing unacceptable infrastructure risk
What We Do

End-to-End Structured Cabling — Design Through Documentation.

From the initial site walk to the as-built drawings handed off at close-out, every engagement covers the full scope.

📐

Cabling System Design

Site survey and structured cabling design covering horizontal runs, backbone topology, MDF and IDF placement, pathway and conduit routing, and outlet counts. Designed to ANSI/TIA-568 and local code — delivered before the first drill hits the wall.

🔌

Cat6A & Cat6 Installation

Horizontal cabling runs to workstations, APs, cameras, VoIP handsets, and any PoE device — properly managed, labeled, and terminated. Cat6A where 10GbE or PoE++ is needed; Cat6 where 1GbE suffices. No mystery runs, no unlabeled ports.

🗄️

MDF / IDF Build-Out

Main and intermediate distribution frame installation — rack or wall-mount, patch panels, cable management, labeling, and grounding. Sized for current density and next-phase growth. Clean builds that the next technician can follow.

✅

Certification & Testing

End-to-end certification of every run with calibrated test equipment — wire map, length, insertion loss, return loss, and NEXT. Fails get replaced, not hidden. Full test reports delivered as part of project close-out documentation.

🗂️

As-Built Documentation

Floor plan with outlet locations, panel schedules, run IDs, and test results — in a format your IT team and next contractor can use. Not a snapshot on someone's phone. Delivered digitally and in print.

🔧

Remediation & Extension

Audit and remediation of inherited or undocumented cabling — identifying failed runs, unlabeled infrastructure, and capacity gaps. Then extending or replacing what's needed to match the current environment's demands.

Why It Matters

Cabling Is the Physical Foundation. Everything Else Runs On It.

TIA-568
All cabling designed and installed to ANSI/TIA-568 standards — the commercial building cabling specification your insurance carrier, auditor, and enterprise clients expect
PoE+
Cat6A installation supports PoE++ (90W) for the current generation of wireless APs, PTZ cameras, and high-draw endpoints — infrastructure that doesn't need replacing in three years
100%
Every run certified — not spot-checked. Full test results for every port in every panel, delivered with the project close-out package
📋

What You Get at Close-Out

Panel schedules with run IDs. Floor plans with outlet locations marked. Certification test results for every port. Rack elevation diagrams for each MDF and IDF. A set of documentation that travels with the building — not with the installer.
