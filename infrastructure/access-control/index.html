ACCESS CONTROL — DESIGN & INSTALLATION

Physical Access Managed
Centrally, Not with Keys.

Keys don't tell you who entered the server room at 2am. They can't be revoked when someone leaves. They can't enforce that only certain people can access certain doors on certain schedules. Modern access control does all of these — and integrates with the identity stack you're already managing.

Schedule a Site Assessment →
The Problem

Physical Security Gaps Are Compliance Gaps. Cyber Insurance Auditors Now Know This.

Physical access to server rooms, networking closets, and sensitive areas is increasingly a question on SOC 2 audits, HIPAA risk assessments, and cyber insurance questionnaires. "We use a key" is no longer an acceptable answer. Access must be logged, controlled by role, and revocable on termination. That requires an access control system, not a locksmith.

✗
No audit trail — no record of who accessed which door, when — required by HIPAA Security Rule and SOC 2
✗
No termination workflow — keys don't get returned, physical access outlasts employment by weeks or months
✗
No role-based restrictions — every employee has the same key, whether they have reason to be in the server room or not
✓
Layer8 approach — role-based access, full audit log, immediate revocation on termination, integrated with your identity directory
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for an Access Control Engagement.

  • New office buildout — access control designed with the rest of the infrastructure
  • SOC 2 or HIPAA audit requiring physical access logging and control
  • Cyber insurance questionnaire asking about server room physical access controls
  • Termination incident where physical access wasn't revoked promptly
  • Scaling from one office to multiple — needing centralized access management
  • Server room, network closet, or sensitive area requiring controlled access
  • Replacing an aging standalone keypad system with a cloud-managed platform
What We Do

Access Control Designed for Compliance, Integration, and Operational Scale.

From a single server room door to an enterprise multi-site deployment — designed to satisfy your compliance requirements and integrate with the identity infrastructure you already run.

🗺️

Access Control Design

Door-by-door access plan — which doors require controlled access, what credential type and hardware is appropriate for each, how access groups map to your org structure, and which schedule restrictions apply. Designed around your compliance requirements and the physical layout of the space.

🔐

Hardware Installation

Card readers, keypads, mobile credential readers, electric strikes, magnetic locks, door position sensors, and request-to-exit devices — installed, wired, and tested. Door hardware coordinated with your facilities or general contractor where new frames or closers are needed. Every door tested before handoff.

☁️

Cloud-Managed Platforms

Cloud-based access control platforms that your IT team can manage without on-site controllers: Brivo, Verkada, Genea, and Openpath. Role-based access groups, schedule configuration, and remote door management from a browser. Access audit logs available on demand for compliance reviews.

🔗

Identity Integration

Integration with your existing identity directory — Azure AD / Entra, Okta, or Google Workspace. When a user is offboarded in your identity provider, their physical access is revoked automatically. No separate deprovisioning workflow, no manual step that gets missed at 5pm on a Friday.

📊

Audit Logging & Reporting

Full access event log — who presented which credential to which door, at what time, and whether access was granted or denied. Export-ready for compliance reviews, security incidents, and auditor requests. Retention configured to meet your compliance framework's requirement.

🏢

Multi-Site Management

Centralized access control across multiple locations — manage all sites from a single platform, with consistent access groups and policies. Grant or revoke a person's access across every site in a single action. No site-by-site coordination when an employee changes role or departs.

Compliance & Integration

Physical Access That Satisfies Your Auditor and Your IT Team.

SOC 2 / HIPAA
Access control designed and documented to satisfy SOC 2 Type II physical access controls and HIPAA Security Rule physical safeguard requirements — audit log, role-based access, and revocation workflow included
Zero-Trust
Physical access aligned to zero-trust principles — least privilege, just-in-time access review, and automatic deprovisioning on directory offboarding for organizations that have adopted zero-trust network architecture
Instant Revoke
Integration with Azure AD, Okta, or Google Workspace means access revocation happens at the identity layer — the same action that locks a laptop also locks the server room door
