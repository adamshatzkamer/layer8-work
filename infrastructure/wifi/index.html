The Problem

Most Wi-Fi Problems Are Installation Problems. The Hardware Is Fine.

Consumer-grade APs placed in hallways, enterprise APs dropped from a ceiling tile without a survey, or a "same number of APs as the old building" spec — these are how wireless networks fail. The fix is rarely more hardware. It's understanding what the RF environment actually looks like before anything goes in the ceiling.

✗
No survey — APs placed by floor plan estimate, dead zones discovered by users
✗
Co-channel interference — too many APs on the same channel competing instead of coordinating
✗
Sticky clients — devices holding onto a distant AP instead of roaming to a closer one
✓
Layer8 approach — survey first, design from data, validate after deployment, document the result
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for a Wireless Engagement.

  • New office buildout — wireless coverage designed before APs are ordered
  • Renovation changing wall layouts and RF propagation
  • Consistent dead zones or roaming complaints in an existing deployment
  • Adding video conferencing rooms requiring reliable wireless handoff
  • Compliance requirement for segmented guest / staff / IoT wireless
  • High-density environments — conference rooms, open floors, warehouses
  • Upgrading to Wi-Fi 6E and needing a clean install
What We Do

Survey, Design, Deploy, Validate — in That Order.

Wireless done right is a process, not a product. Every engagement follows the same sequence regardless of scale.

📡

Predictive & Active Site Survey

Predictive survey using floor plan modeling before deployment to establish AP count and placement. Active walk-through survey on existing environments — mapping RSSI, channel utilization, interference sources, and coverage gaps. Output: heat maps and placement recommendations your installation can follow.

🗺️

RF Design & AP Placement

AP placement designed from the survey — count, mounting height, antenna orientation, and channel plan. Designed to support your client density, application mix (voice, video, data), and any compliance-required segmentation. No over-deployment, no gaps.

🔧

Enterprise AP Deployment

Physical installation of access points — ceiling, wall, or outdoor — on the Cat6A or fiber drops the design calls for. Managed and controller-based deployments configured for roaming, band steering, SSID segmentation, and QoS. We work with Cisco Meraki, Ubiquiti, Aruba, and Extreme.

🔒

Network Segmentation & VLANs

Guest, staff, IoT, and voice SSIDs mapped to separate VLANs with appropriate firewall policy. Required for HIPAA-covered wireless environments, PCI-DSS compliance, and cyber insurance questionnaires that ask whether IoT devices are network-isolated.

✅

Post-Deployment Validation

Active survey after installation — confirming coverage, signal levels, channel plan, and roaming behavior match the design. Findings addressed before project close-out. Validation report delivered with the documentation package.

📈

Optimization & Remediation

Audit and optimization of existing wireless environments — channel and power tuning, AP repositioning, firmware updates, and roaming configuration. For environments where the hardware is fine but the configuration is not.
