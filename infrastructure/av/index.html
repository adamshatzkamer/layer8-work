AUDIO / VIDEO — DESIGN & INTEGRATION

Conference Rooms That Work
Every Time.

The meeting starts in two minutes. Someone can't get the display to switch inputs. The far end can't hear anyone. The camera is aimed at the ceiling. These problems are infrastructure problems, not user problems — and they're fully avoidable with a proper AV design and a clean installation.

Schedule a Consultation →
The Problem

Bad AV Is a Productivity Tax Charged at the Start of Every Meeting.

Conference rooms designed by whoever pulled the cable, with consumer displays, a Bluetooth speakerphone, and a laptop HDMI that never quite fits — this is how most mid-market offices end up with meeting rooms nobody wants to use. Hybrid work amplified every one of these problems. The remote participants are the ones who pay the most.

✗
No control system — five remotes, a cable drawer, and a 10-minute setup ritual before every call
✗
Room echo and pickup gaps — audio designed for one seat, unusable from the rest of the table
✗
Platform lock-in problems — room hardware certified for Zoom only, org moved to Teams, nothing works
✓
Layer8 approach — one-touch rooms designed for hybrid work, documented, and tested before handoff
When to Call Us

Common Triggers for an AV Engagement.

  • New office buildout with conference rooms to design from scratch
  • Renovating or repurposing existing spaces for hybrid meetings
  • Executive boardroom requiring professional-grade audio and video
  • Moving to Zoom Rooms or Microsoft Teams Rooms hardware
  • All-hands or training space needing display and audio distribution
  • Digital signage for lobbies, wayfinding, or operations dashboards
  • Existing rooms that nobody uses because they don't work reliably
What We Do

Conference Rooms to All-Hands Spaces — Designed for Hybrid Work.

Every engagement is scoped to the room's use case, not a spec sheet pulled from a catalog. From a four-person huddle room to a 200-seat auditorium.

🖥️

Conference Room Design & Integration

Room layout, display sizing and placement, camera field-of-view, microphone coverage mapping, and speaker placement — designed for the specific room dimensions and seating configuration. One-touch operation from a room controller or touch panel. Works with Zoom Rooms, Teams Rooms, and BYOD.

🎙️

Audio Design & DSP

Ceiling microphone arrays, tabletop conference units, and DSP configuration to cover the full table — not just the seat nearest the speakerphone. Echo cancellation, noise suppression, and mix-minus configured for intelligible far-end audio. No acoustic compromise between in-room and remote participants.

📺

Display & Video Systems

Commercial-grade displays selected for brightness, viewing angle, and format — not consumer TVs re-purposed for conference rooms. LED, LCD, and projection options for rooms from 10 to 10,000 square feet. Display control, input switching, and content distribution engineered as part of the room system.

🏢

Digital Signage

Lobby displays, wayfinding systems, operations dashboards, and company communication screens — cloud-managed content with a schedule and update workflow that doesn't require IT involvement for every change. Hardware selected for commercial-duty continuous operation.

🔌

AV Infrastructure & Cabling

HDMI, HDBaseT, and fiber AV distribution; structured cabling for AV endpoints; conduit and cable management for clean, serviceable installs. Coordinated with the data cabling plan so AV and IT infrastructure share pathways without conflict.

📋

Training & Documentation

End-user training for room operation — how to start a meeting, share content, adjust audio. System documentation for IT and facilities: rack diagrams, equipment schedules, configuration backups, and a maintenance guide. Rooms that your team can actually support after we leave.

Platform Support

Platform-Agnostic. Certified for the Rooms You're Running.

Zoom & Teams
Zoom Rooms and Microsoft Teams Rooms certified hardware design and deployment — Poly, Logitech, Neat, Crestron, and Biamp certified platforms
Any Size
From four-person huddle rooms to executive boardrooms to all-hands auditoriums — scoped to the room, not a catalog template
One Touch
Room control systems configured so any employee can start a meeting without IT support — display on, camera on, call launched from a single tap
🎯

Tested Before Handoff

Every room is tested with a live call before the project closes — not handed off with a disclaimer about needing a few more tweaks. Remote participants can hear every seat. Every seat can see the display. The control panel works the way it was designed. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't close.
