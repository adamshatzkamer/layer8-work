The Problem

It Belongs to Everyone, So It Belongs to No One.

Every owner already knows this is happening. What they usually cannot work out is whose job it is to fix.

🌙

The contact arrives when you're closed

Evenings, weekends, holidays, the middle of a job. That is when people have time to call you and fill in your form — and it is exactly when there is no one at the desk to take it.

🚪

It isn't any one department's problem

Sales says it's reception. Reception says it's after hours. Operations says it's a sales lead. The caller sits in the gap between three teams that each have a reason it isn't theirs.

🔁

So it never gets solved

It gets raised at a meeting, gets a voicemail greeting bolted onto it, and comes back six months later. Nothing structural changes because no one owns the whole path from ring to booked.

→

Why we sell this as one thing

A voice agent that answers but does not write to your CRM just moves the dropped ball. A reminder system with nothing feeding it has nothing to remind anyone about. The reason missed inbound survives every partial fix is that the fixes are partial. This is deployed as a single path, end to end, or it reproduces the same gap somewhere further down.

What Gets Installed

One System. Six Working Parts.

Not a menu to choose from — this is the path a single inbound contact travels, from the moment it arrives to the review request weeks later. Each part hands off to the next.

1

AI voice attendant

Answers the call. Every call, at any hour, with no queue and no voicemail. It handles the conversation in natural speech — what the caller needs, when they need it, how to reach them — and it does not hang up on anyone because the office is shut.

2

24/7 lead capture

Turns that conversation into a record. Name, number, what they wanted, when they called. The same applies to web forms and messages arriving overnight — every lead captured as a structured entry rather than a notification someone has to remember to act on.

3

CRM and workflow automation

Files the record where your team already works and routes it to whoever should own it. This is the part that closes the departmental gap — the lead lands with a named owner and a next action, so it is no longer sitting between three teams waiting to be claimed.

4

SMS reminders

Confirms the appointment and reminds ahead of it, by text, without anyone making the call. The booking that came in at 7pm on Saturday gets the same reminder treatment as one taken at the desk on a Tuesday.

5

Automated review solicitation

Asks for the review after the work is done — every customer, every time, at the point where they are most likely to say yes. Not a campaign someone remembers to run; a step in the path that fires on its own.

6

Smart follow-up

Picks up the ones that did not close. The enquiry that never booked, the quote that went quiet, the customer who has not been back — followed up on a schedule, so the contact you worked to capture is not abandoned one step short of the outcome.

✓

What we are claiming

Every call answered. Every lead captured. Every review requested. Those are descriptions of what the system does, not predictions about what it will earn you — your close rate, your pricing, and your service quality still determine the result. We will not quote you a number we cannot stand behind.

Deployment

Installed in Days. Not a Phase Plan.

There is no discovery workstream, no future-state architecture, no steering committee. We take your number, your calendar, and your CRM, configure the path against how you actually work, and switch it on.

You keep your phone number. Your team keeps the tools they already use. The change your staff notices is that the overnight calls are waiting for them in the morning, already written down and already assigned.

→

We configure against your operation

How your calls should be handled, who owns which kind of enquiry, what your booking rules are, and when follow-up should fire.

→

We connect it to what you run

Your existing number, calendar, and CRM. No migration and no replacement of systems that are working.

→

We run it live and adjust

It goes on, real calls come through it, and we tune the handling against what actually happens rather than what we assumed.

Tooling and Scope

Where This Runs.

🔐

Enterprise tenancy, commercial terms

This runs on enterprise-tenant tooling operated under a commercial agreement — not on consumer accounts and not on free tiers. Anything that would put the system in contact with regulated data is scoped separately before it is built, not assumed into the standard deployment.
