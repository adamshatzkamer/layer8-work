Capabilities

From Discovery to Deployed Automation.

Every Layer8 AI engagement starts with what's actually happening in your business — then moves to prioritized implementation, not a roadmap that sits in a drawer.

🔎

AI Use Audit & Inventory

A structured discovery of AI tools currently in use across your organization — including tools your IT team doesn't know about. We map what's being used, by whom, for what purpose, and against what data. Output: a complete AI tool inventory your compliance, insurance, and security teams can use.

🗺️

Use Case Prioritization

Structured identification of where AI creates the most leverage in your specific business — scored by ROI potential, implementation complexity, data sensitivity, and adoption likelihood. We surface the 3–5 use cases worth building first, not a list of 40 things you'll never get to.

⚙️

Workflow Automation Design & Build

Hands-on implementation of AI-powered workflows — n8n, Make, Zapier, custom GPT builders, Microsoft Copilot, and direct API integrations. We design the workflow, build the automation, test it against your real data, and document it so your team owns it after we leave.

📋

AI Acceptable Use Policy

A policy your team will actually read and follow — not a 40-page compliance document. Covers approved tools, prohibited data categories, output validation requirements, client confidentiality obligations, and escalation paths. Calibrated for your vertical and your carrier's AI questionnaire requirements.

🏛️

AI Governance Framework

A governance structure for ongoing AI decisions — how new tools get evaluated and approved, how AI outputs get validated before client delivery, how AI incidents get reported, and who owns AI risk in your organization. Built to scale as AI expands in your business.

📊

ROI Measurement & Reporting

Most AI implementations don't get measured. Layer8 builds the measurement framework before implementation — defining what success looks like, what data to capture, and how to report AI ROI to leadership and the board. Your AI investment becomes defensible, not just directionally positive.
