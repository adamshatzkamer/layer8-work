The Problem

Technology Decisions Made Without a Current-State View Create Expensive Surprises.

Without a structured assessment, technology investment decisions get made on the basis of whoever is loudest, whatever broke most recently, or what a vendor is selling. The result is a patchwork of tools that don't integrate, security gaps that compound over time, and a leadership team that can't answer basic questions about their technology environment.

No technology inventory — critical systems aren't documented; knowledge lives with individual employees

✗

✓
Layer8 assessment — an independent, structured view of what you have, what's at risk, and what to invest in first
When It Makes Sense

The Right Time to Get an Independent Technology View.

Technology assessments are highest-value at inflection points — when the status quo is clearly not sustainable, and before major investment decisions are made. The most common triggers:

  • Preparing for a transaction — buy-side or sell-side
  • Post-acquisition integration planning
  • First technology leadership hire (before they start)
  • Major vendor contract coming up for renewal
  • Cybersecurity incident or near-miss
  • Rapid headcount growth straining current systems
  • Board or investor diligence on technology posture
What We Assess

Eight Domains. Scored. Prioritized. Actionable.

A Layer8 technology assessment covers the domains that matter for mid-market companies — infrastructure, security, identity, applications, data, AI, vendor relationships, and strategic alignment.

🏗️

Infrastructure & Architecture

Current-state documentation of your infrastructure footprint — cloud environments, on-premise systems, network architecture, and connectivity. Scored against reliability, scalability, and cost efficiency benchmarks for companies your size.

🔐

Security Posture

Evaluation of your security controls against the criteria your insurers, auditors, and enterprise clients actually check — MFA, endpoint protection, access management, backup architecture, patch management, logging, and incident response readiness.

👤

Identity & Access Management

Review of how identities are managed across your organization — provisioning and deprovisioning, SSO coverage, privileged access, device management, and directory structure. Identity gaps are the most common source of both security incidents and operational friction.

📦

Application Portfolio

Inventory and assessment of your core business applications — integration architecture, duplication, vendor health, licensing efficiency, and alignment to current business processes. Identifies both consolidation opportunities and critical dependencies.

🤖

AI & Automation Posture

Inventory of AI tools in use, governance gaps, data exposure risks, and prioritized automation opportunities. Produces the AI tool inventory required by current cyber insurance questionnaires and compliance frameworks.

📊

Strategic Alignment & Roadmap

Assessment of how well your current technology stack supports your business objectives — and what investment priorities would unlock the most value in the next 12–24 months. Deliverable: a board-ready roadmap with cost estimates, sequencing, and rationale.
