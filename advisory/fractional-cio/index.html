The In-Between Problem

Too Big to Operate Without Leadership.
Too Small to Justify a Full-Time Executive.

At $5M–$50M revenue, technology decisions carry real risk. A bad vendor contract, a failed migration, a compliance gap, or a security incident doesn't just slow the business — it can define it. But the answer isn't a $250K salary and a five-year employment commitment.

The fractional model gives you the same quality of leadership — strategy, roadmap, vendor management, board reporting — without the overhead. You pay for the days you need, not the full-time seat.

⚠

No One Owns Technology Strategy

Decisions are made reactively — by the most technical employee, by a vendor, or not at all. The business operates without a roadmap.

⚠

Vendor Relationships Are Unmanaged

Contracts auto-renew, scope creep goes unchallenged, and the business has no independent advocate when something goes wrong.

⚠

Board and Investors Have No Technology Visibility

Technology risk isn't being reported. Leadership can't answer basic questions about infrastructure, security posture, or technical debt.

What We Do

What a Fractional CIO/CTO Does for Your Business.

The full scope of technology leadership — delivered at the level of engagement your business actually requires.

🗺️

Technology Strategy & Roadmap

Current-state assessment, gap identification, and a prioritized roadmap aligned to your business goals and budget. Updated quarterly — not a one-time document that sits in a drawer.

🤝

Vendor Management

Contract review, renewal negotiations, performance management, and selection for new vendors. You have an independent advocate — not someone the vendor has already sold.

👥

Technology Team Oversight

Hire, mentor, and manage your IT staff and MSP. Set standards, review work, and build a team that scales with the business rather than one that limits it.

📊

Board & Executive Reporting

Technology risk, investment, and performance reported in business terms. Board members and investors get the visibility they need; you get a prepared, credible spokesperson.

🔒

Security & Compliance Oversight

Security posture assessment, policy ownership, and compliance monitoring. HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI, and cyber insurance requirements tracked and managed — not delegated and forgotten.

🚀

Project & Initiative Leadership

Major migrations, integrations, and platform changes led by someone who has done it before — not managed by a vendor with a financial interest in scope expansion.

Ideal Engagement

When to Bring In a Fractional CIO/CTO.

→

$5M–$50M Revenue, No Tech Executive

The business is large enough to have real technology risk but not large enough to justify the fully-loaded cost of a senior technology executive.

→

Post-Acquisition or Merger

Two technology environments need to be rationalized. Timelines are short, stakes are high, and the internal team doesn't have the bandwidth or the experience.

→

Compliance or Audit Pressure

A customer, insurer, or regulator is asking questions your team can't answer credibly. You need someone who has been on both sides of that conversation.

→

Technology Executive Transition

Your CTO or IT director left or is leaving. You need continuity, a credible search process, and someone to hold the standard while you find the right permanent hire.

Engagement Structure

How the Engagement Works.

1–2 Days Per Month

Strategic oversight — roadmap maintenance, vendor reviews, board reporting, and executive advising. Right for businesses that need the seat filled without hands-on implementation work.

3–4 Days Per Month

Active leadership — everything above plus project oversight, team management, and initiative execution. Right for businesses in a transition, growth phase, or compliance push.

Project-Based

Fixed-scope engagement around a specific initiative — migration, compliance framework, vendor selection, or post-acquisition rationalization. SOW-driven with defined deliverables.
