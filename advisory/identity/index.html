The Identity Gap

Most SMBs Have Accounts.
Not Access Controls.

There's a meaningful difference between having user accounts in Microsoft 365 and having a managed identity environment. The former is a collection of usernames and passwords. The latter is a governed system where access is conditional, devices are managed, and every login is verified against policy.

Cyber insurers, HIPAA auditors, and SOC 2 assessors are all asking for the latter. Most SMBs show them the former and hope no one looks too closely.

⚠

No MFA Enforcement

MFA is available but not enforced. Some users have it, some don't. Insurers and auditors want coverage — not availability.

⚠

Unmanaged Devices Accessing Company Data

Personal phones and laptops connect to company email and files with no device management policy, no encryption enforcement, and no remote wipe capability.

⚠

No Lifecycle Management

Former employees retain access for weeks after departure. Contractors and vendors accumulate permissions over time. No formal provisioning or deprovisioning process exists.
